ssh-detect-respond

Python · github.com/zellerbrook/ssh-detect-respond

Parses SSH authentication logs into structured events (timestamp, source IP, username, outcome) and tails live files, surviving log rotation and in-place truncation. That is the failure mode where a naive monitor goes silently blind while still looking healthy. It is the log-ingestion layer underneath brute-force detection tools like fail2ban and CrowdSec. The pytest suite covers parsing, rotation, and truncation.
